How it works

Three steps. You own all of it.

01 — THE BUILD

Your page, built and proven

We build your direct-booking page on The Continental — our own booking system — in your colors, with your rooms, your rates, your policies. Connected to your own Stripe account. Tested end to end before it goes live. Fixed price, fixed date.

02 — YOURS

You get the keys

The page runs on a monthly subscription: tape-chart calendar, payments, reports, guest list — all yours. Manage it yourself, or let us drive. Cancel and you take every guest record with you. Your data leaves with you — one click, always.

03 — GROW

We fill it

With Managed Growth we run the Google and Meta ads, watch the numbers, adjust rates seasonally, and sit down with you monthly. The headline of every report: commission you didn't pay.
